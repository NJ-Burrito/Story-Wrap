# The calm format: creative rules

⭐ Reference: **E01 calm v5** (approved by the owner, 2026-10-25, `work/episodes/E01C`). His pacing, our art. Build steps and checks: `pipelines/CALM.md`. Rule ids: `kb/rulebook.md` section 15. Each line below is DO or NEVER, with the reason and the note that created it.

## 🎞️ Pacing and staging

- ✅ **Calm shots of about 3.7 s, one subject per frame, deliberate holds allowed.** Why: the reference reels are slow on purpose; the viewer reads and listens. NEVER cut to prove energy (the "never a slideshow" cut thresholds do not apply here; the art checks do).
- ✅ **Let the story set the length.** E01C is 127 s. Why: the owner decided (2026-10-25) that any length is fine: no cap, no target; only a soft warning over 92 s asks him to confirm. NEVER pad or trim to hit a number, and ignore older advice about reel length.
- ✅ **Open on the story.** E01 opens on the bag at 0 s with no recap; recaps of the last vote start at E02. Why: E01 has no vote.
- ✅ **A prop close-up gets real depth** (`insert_depth`): far set, counter, the prop with a flat 2D shadow, a dark occluder, a slow dolly. NEVER a close-up on one flat plane. Why: flat inserts failed the parallax check and a wider pan broke the calm motion band.
- ✅ **Readability in a close-up comes from the camera.** NEVER enlarge a hand, face or prop to read better (Hero's hands, 2026-10-25). Why: the art book is the reference every time (`ART-PROPORTION`).
- ✅ **Hands apart between unrelated men and women**; an object is handed over, never a touch. Why: the owner, shot 2.

## 🧊 Introductions (C10)

- ✅ **The first time a character appears as a person: a dramatic cut, the frame freezes, a grey rim isolates them, the rest dims, hand-drawn graffiti gives their name and one line from the bible.** Why: the viewer needs the world explained in one beat. NEVER introduce twice; a prop close-up or a bare hand is not an appearance.
- ✅ **Keep it short and readable**: name at most 2 words, tag at most 6, the text fully drawn before the beat ends, 3 to 9 s. The narrator may read it. The music dips.

## 🗣️ Narrator and characters

- ✅ **One firm narrator speaking to the audience as «أنتم»**, present tense, short sentences, carrying the story; characters add short lines in their approved voices. Why: the reference reels address the viewer; a close-third narrator was a fault. NEVER a narrator voice whose reference is noisy, gated or boxy (C2).
- ✅ **Voice first.** Scene length comes from the takes. One take per line, no re-voice; new words are a new line.
- ✅ **Mouths move with the words (C6)** for drawn speakers only; the narrator has no mouth, and a listener never moves theirs as if talking.
- ✅ **A written object is read in a different register** (C3): the horn, an iris onto the plate, the narrator flat and distinct, the caption engraved.

## 🔤 Captions

- ✅ **Show the FULL sentence and highlight the word being spoken** (C7). NEVER single words flashing by.
- ✅ **Keep it up long enough to read** (C12): at least 1.5 s and characters over 12 a second; add a pause rather than shorten the plate.
- ✅ **Gloss EVERY cultural or own-language word in Arabic, in brackets, for as long as the sentence is up** (C8, C11): «شيكامو (تحية احترام للكبار)». NEVER only the words the owner pointed at; unknown words go to a reader.
- ✅ **Letters stay joined** (C1): the outline is a separate layer under the fills.
- ✅ **Captions sit away from faces** and inside the safe area, one plate at a time. NEVER cover a character's eyes or glasses.
- ❌ NEVER tashkeel on anything people see.

## 🔊 Sound

- ✅ **Every transition has a picture AND a sound, chosen together** (C3). NEVER a silent join or a sound with no picture.
- ✅ **Every pen stroke, key, card, bag, step, cloth and lamp gets a foley cue** (C5), at a level set relative to the voice, never from the file's peak.
- ✅ **Mix for a phone** (C9): voice first, a bed that a phone speaker can play, ducked under speech, -14 LUFS. NEVER judge the mix on headphones or by a file's peak.
- ❌ NEVER let a cue or the bed eat a word (clarity check).

## 🔀 The choice and the comments

- ✅ **Never end on a situation: end on a choice** (C4). Question and two choices in the bottom half; the top half keeps moving, cutting between two close-ups of the two answers, with a pop and a VFX at each cut, in time with the narrator.
- ✅ **Two choices, each a short phrase a viewer can type** (at most 4 words, no digits).
- ✅ **Ask a question, not for a typed answer** (DESIGN.md 5.2, replaces C13's «write your choice in your own words», 2026-10-25): from E02 the closing line and the card prompt are an open question about the story with a reason («أين تضعون الحقيبة الليلة؟ قولوا لنا لماذا.», card «ما رأيكم، ولماذا؟»). NEVER "comment 1 or 2", never «اكتبوا اختياركم», «علقوا», «صوتوا», never a request to type a given word. Why: Instagram and Facebook demote posts that ask for a typed word, number or vote; the vote still counts by wording, with at least 8 `variants` per side.
- ✅ **The follow card states a fact**: «الحلقة القادمة تتبع ما تكتبونه», not «تابعوا لتعرفوا» (a follow-for-part-2 plea).
- ✅ **The first seconds are the hook** (from E02): the recap card is spoken, the narrator says the vote result in «أنتم» (≤ 8 words, ≤ 3.0 s, first voiced line before 3 s), the first scene line names the heroine, and the HUD carries «الحلقة N · the series» for 3 s.
- ✅ **A small NJ mark in the top-right corner of every frame** (the owner 2026-10-25): faint cream, clear of every caption plate, HUD, face and card, outside the phone UI zones; laid in the last compose step.
- ✅ **The next episode adapts the winning branch;** the losing branch is parked, never deleted.

## 🔎 What a person still checks

Lip sync by eye, the phone mix by ear, arm motion and grip changes, the graffiti against the character's head. These pass the machine checks but were not looked at on E01C v5.

## Format v2: characters carry the story (the owner 2026-10-28, after E03 was rejected)

- ✅ **Characters speak the story in their own cast voices**: 10 to 15 spoken lines per episode, at least 2 characters answering each other. Why: E03 had 19 of 19 narrator lines and no character voice; the owner: "no narration voices for the characters". NEVER narrate what a character says, thinks or does.
- ✅ **The narrator is a link, not the story**: the recap, a short "that night..." link between nights (one sentence, at most 14 words) and the vote question, at most 25 % of the voice time. Voice of any kind still fills at least 0.85 of the runtime.
- ✅ **2 to 5 events, each SHOWN**: a character does something visible (closes the drain, opens a box, finds an object, argues, runs) in its own action scene. NEVER tell it, and a reaction close-up alone is not an event.
- ✅ **Every night has a real event.** NEVER a night of a character looking at an empty box.
- ✅ **Intros once per series.** A character's freeze happens at their first appearance in the whole series only; later episodes never re-introduce and never spend scenes explaining who someone is.
- ✅ **Missing art is created.** If an event needs a prop, pose or set piece the kits lack, draw it (art-book style), add it to the catalogue and list it in the plan's `needs_art`. NEVER avoid the action because the art does not exist.
- ✅ **Dialogue may breathe**: a dialogue scene can run up to about 10 s; keep soft joins and holds.
