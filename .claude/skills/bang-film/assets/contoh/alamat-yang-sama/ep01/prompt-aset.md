# Prompt aset — Alamat yang Sama · Ep 01

Generate aset ini dulu di AI gambar mana pun. Simpan dengan nama berkas yang tertulis di tiap
judul, karena nama itu yang dipakai sebagai token di `prompt-video.md`.

Aset baru di episode ini: ADIT, TERAS, RUANG TENGAH, MOTOR. Ruang tengah sudah dibutuhkan di
ep 01 karena shot terakhir mengintip ke dalamnya lewat celah pintu.

---

## Sheet karakter · ADIT
Simpan sebagai: `adit_sheet.png` → token `[gambar_adit]`

```
Character reference sheet for film production. Three panels side by side, separated by
thin subtle vertical gaps. One continuous neutral dark grey seamless studio backdrop
across all three panels. Soft even studio lighting, photorealistic cinematic photography,
35mm film look, fine grain, muted desaturated colour.

LEFT PANEL: tight close-up portrait, head and shoulders only, neutral expression, mouth
closed, eyes looking straight into the lens, sharp focus on the eyes.

MIDDLE PANEL: the SAME person, full body head to feet, standing straight facing the
camera, head and face fully visible, arms relaxed at the sides, feet together, neutral
expression.

RIGHT PANEL: the SAME person, full body head to feet, standing straight seen from
directly behind, head fully visible, arms relaxed at the sides, showing the back of the
clothing.

THE PERSON: Indonesian man, 27 years old, 170 cm, sun-darkened brown skin, short black hair flattened
on top from wearing a helmet, narrow face with slightly hollow cheeks, a small dark mole on
his left cheekbone, wiry thin build.

WARDROBE: faded green ride-hailing driver jacket with grey reflective strips and no logos
or lettering, plain grey crew-neck t-shirt underneath, dark blue jeans worn pale at the
knees, worn black canvas sneakers, a black phone pouch on a strap across his chest.

A clean white sans-serif label reading ADIT printed in the lower left corner of the left
panel.

The same person, the same face, the same clothing in all three panels. Photorealistic,
not illustration.

Avoid: more than one person per panel, different faces between panels, missing head,
patterned or gradient background, hard shadows, dramatic camera angles, extra text,
watermark, logo.
```

## Set plate · Teras
Simpan sebagai: `teras_plate.png` → token `[gambar_teras]`

```
Photorealistic exterior photograph, absolutely no people in frame.

Front terrace of a small single-storey house in a narrow inner-Jakarta residential lane.
A frosted-glass aluminium sliding door with a thin curtain behind it and a one-finger gap
along the bottom edge. Low green-painted iron fence and gate, potted snake plants along
the wall. Old grey terrazzo floor tiles under an even, undisturbed layer of fine pale
dust. Beside the door, a doorbell button with peeling cream paint and its wire hanging
loose.

Midday, hard sun almost directly overhead, short shadows falling straight down. 24mm lens,
eye height, level horizon, straight-on composition from the lane looking at the door.

Avoid: people, text, signage, house numbers, packages, footprints in the dust, extra
furniture, coloured practical lights, lens flare, watermark.
```
