VIDEO STYLE & FORMAT
30-second scene in the style of a modern Indonesian short drama. ONE continuous moment — a
single midday delivery to one house, NOT a trailer, NOT a montage — covered like a real
film: cut between MULTIPLE camera positions, angles changing every few seconds while the
moment flows continuously. Quiet, patient pacing; long stillness is intended. Naturalistic,
underplayed acting. Look: warm-neutral filmic colour grading, fine film grain, shallow
depth of field, accurate Indonesian skin tones, bright midday light that never blows out.
This is a quiet family drama, NOT horror and NOT a thriller. NOT sinetron, NO dramatic
zooms, NO jump scares, NO rapid cutting.

LANGUAGE
All spoken words in INDONESIAN (Bahasa Indonesia), Jakarta register, casual spoken form —

below: do not translate them, do not paraphrase them, do not invent extra lines. Exactly

muttering, no repeating his line early. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text of
any kind burned into the frame. Character names are never spoken aloud unless they appear

OPTICS

up to 85mm (FOV approx 24 degrees) to isolate Adit from what is behind him. The opening
floor shot and the final view through the gap use a 50mm. Per-shot lens is specified in
the breakdown. No wide-angle distortion, no fisheye, no zoom moves.
