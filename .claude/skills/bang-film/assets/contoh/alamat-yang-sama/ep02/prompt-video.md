VIDEO STYLE & FORMAT
16-second scene in the style of a modern Indonesian short drama. ONE continuous moment —

real film: cut between MULTIPLE camera positions while the moment flows continuously.

warm-neutral filmic colour grading, fine film grain, shallow depth of field, accurate

sinetron, NO dramatic zooms, NO jump scares, NO rapid cutting.

LANGUAGE
This scene has NO spoken dialogue — no voices, no narration, no inner monologue, no
whispering. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text of any kind burned into the

OPTICS

Every cut moves to a DIFFERENT physical position — the far corner of the room, beside the
stack of boxes, behind the floral sofa, and in front of the cabinet at the height of the
