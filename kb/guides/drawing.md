# Drawing: four-level ladder for the ink art book

Source: `research/01` (URLs there). Caveat: Drawabox official lessons, Loomis and Vilppu texts, and most Proko lessons were not read; points rest on public excerpts, student critiques and secondary summaries [S]. Levels are cumulative. Rules: R-070 to R-080. Medium here: black-and-white ink only (CLAUDE).

## Level 1: Basics

- Gesture first: one action line (C, S or straight), detail is nothing without it; asymmetry avoids the stiff "snowman" [S https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-gesture].
- Construction before contour: spheres, boxes, cylinders carry perspective and overlap [S https://nma.art/drawingmanual].
- Value is plane change: block the shadow shape before any blending [S https://proko.com/course-lesson/elements-of-light-and-shadow].
- Controlled, single-pass line; hatching follows form [S Drawabox critiques; Virtual Instructor].
- Checks: the pose reads from the action line alone; single confident lines; light and shadow shapes separated; no accidental symmetry; hatching follows form.

## Level 2: Intermediate

- Proportion is a range: about 7.5 heads average, 8 ideal, 8.5 heroic; chosen per character, no group canon [S https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_proportions].
- Horizon equals eye level; vanishing points on it [S Design Sketchbook].
- Five-value light: three lights, two shadows; core shadow darkest along the terminator; reflected light stays in the shadow family [S Proko].
- Line weight carries depth: heavier near and on the shadow side; flexible G-type nib for contours, fine maru pen for detail [S https://www.too.com/manga/en/inking.html].
- Hatching: parallel, even spacing, tighter reads darker; cross-hatch only in the darkest areas; many directions look like "a big mess" [S Bluprint].
- Checks: one head unit per figure; one horizon; five steps in order; deliberate weight; one hatch direction.

## Level 3: Advanced

- Hands: palm box plus finger shapes, thumb separate; the mitten simplification [S Proko; Austin CC handout].
- Faces: expression by masses (brow, cheek, mouth) before line; seven emotions as a ladder drill [S Proko 12 Days].
- Folds: mark tension and compression points on the body first; pipe folds [S ArtWod; Clip Studio].
- Value composition: three-value thumbnails, choose a key (high or low) before detail, squint to find shapes [S Proko].
- Blacks: overdraw in passes to avoid light patches; brush pens for large areas; white ink for fixes; frame lines about 0.5 to 0.8 mm [S Too.com].
- Checks: hands read at thumbnail; faces read at a squint; every fold has a visible cause; a focal area and a key chosen; even blacks.

## Level 4: Mastery

- Character-first proportion from the bible.
- Light tells the story: one light system, cast shadows agree.
- Depth by value and line weight, not blur.
- Composition by shape; crop-proof for 9:16.
- Style is a system: one line policy, one hatch policy, one black policy, one key across the series (a design judgement [J]).
- Eye level is a story choice (low for power, high for vulnerability) [J, not source-backed].
- Checks: each figure has its own proportion sheet; one light direction per scene; ink policy consistent across a sequence; the story point reads at thumbnail and at 9:16.

## Pipeline checklist (pass/fail, also for AI-assisted plates)

| Level | Check | Pass test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pose from action line | Hide the outline; the pose still reads |
| 1 | Line quality | No multi-stroke wobble on clean lines |
| 2 | Head unit | One per figure |
| 2 | Horizon | All vanishing points on one horizon |
| 2 | Value steps | Five in order |
| 2 | Hatching | One main direction |
| 3 | Hands and faces | Read at thumbnail |
| 3 | Blacks | Even |
| 4 | Ink policy | Same across frames |
| 4 | Crop | Story point holds at 9:16 |

## Ink policy (write once as `artbook/INK-POLICY.md`, R-078)

Decide before any plate: nib or brush look for contours, thin line for interior, hatch direction and spacing steps, where solid black goes, whether dots (screentone style) are allowed, frame line weight. Everything is black on white; no grey fills (R-070).

## Gaps

Two-point perspective, facial-muscle anatomy and screentone have thin sources; read a dedicated lesson before relying on them (Q-12).
