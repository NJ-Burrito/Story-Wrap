# Rulebook

One list of rules, grouped by stage. Numbers never change: a rule that is merged or replaced stays in place as `retired -> R-xxx`. A rule that was changed by a later one carries `Amended by R-xxx`.

Columns: **Rule** (one line) | **Why** (the F-note, root cause or source it came from) | **Check** (a gate check id, or `human look` when no machine check exists).

How to read the check column
- SPEC, RIG, RENDER (`extras`, `render`), LOOK2D, HANDS, PREVIEW name the gate command that prints `FAIL [ID]`. Thresholds and calibration status: `kb/quality/gate-spec.md`. Pipeline commands: `pipelines/RUNBOOK.md`.
- `human look` = a person (owner, or a Sonnet reviewer seat) looks at a contact sheet or the video. No gate can see it.
- Grades on claims: V verified, S secondary, M measured, J judgement (`kb/README.md`). Rules marked (CLAUDE) are the owner's own words in `CLAUDE.md`.
- F-numbers are the owner's notes, verbatim in `bible/feedback/2026-10-23-mix-v2.md`. The craft file under each heading holds the long text, sources and procedures. The failure table (F-note, what the owner saw, root cause, rule, check) is in `bible/craft/README.md`.

Stage map: 1 Global | 2 Research | 3 Writing | 4 Sketching (art book) | then the shot pipeline `init, catalogue, camera, spec, build, trace, record, gate, review` | 5 Catalogue | 6 Camera | 7 Spec | 8 Build | 9 Record | 10 Gate | 11 Review | 12 Preview | 13 Composing | 14 Retired and amended | 15 Calm format.
