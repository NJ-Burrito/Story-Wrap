# Art-book character spread (two facing pages)

Design template [J, adapted from research/05]. Black-and-white ink only (R-070). Page and pixel sizes are set when the book is laid out. File: `artbook/characters/<slug>-spread.md` plus plates.

| Zone | Page | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A. Hero plate | Left | Full or near-bleed ink figure, best pose, silhouette-tested |
| B. Turnaround | Right | Front, side, back, three-quarter in one row aligned on guidelines |
| C. Head study | Right | Three expressions: neutral, the extreme trait, one reaction |
| D. Props | Right | Two or three props at scale, each with a one-line label |
| E. Caption block | Left | Name, age, origin, the extreme trait in one sentence |
| F. Shape notes | Left | Thin ink diagram of the dominant shape family and the silhouette result |

## Rules
- One hero per spread. Labels are a few words.
- The trait sentence equals the bible's sentence word for word (R-079).
- Silhouette sheet attached: idle, action, reaction, solid black (R-071).
- Thumbnail B&W identification against the rest of the cast succeeds (R-072).
- Costume elements at most 3, each linked to a bible field (R-074).
- Applies `artbook/INK-POLICY.md` (R-078); holds as a 9:16 crop (R-080).
- Culture reviewer sign-off or "pending" recorded on the spread file (R-041, R-043).
