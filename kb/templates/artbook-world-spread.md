# Art-book world spread (two facing pages)

Design template [J, adapted from research/05]. Black-and-white ink only (R-070). File: `artbook/worlds/<slug>-spread.md` plus plates.

| Zone | Page | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A. Establishing plate | Left | Full-bleed ink landscape or interior, one light source, dark masses |
| B. Scale strip | Right | Three human-scale references: a character, a door, a vehicle |
| C. Architecture or nature study | Right | Two details of the place: material and structure |
| D. Culture cues | Left | Objects, signs, clothing of the place, each linked to a bible entry |
| E. Caption block | Left | Place name, location, one sentence of function, the story's rule for it |

## Rules
- The establishing plate sets the value key for the whole world; one light, one horizon (R-076, R-077).
- Cue objects are specific, never generic; each links to a world bible field and passes the stereotype list (R-043).
- Ink policy applied (R-078); holds as a 9:16 crop (R-080).
- Power or technology of the world is visible somewhere in the plates (R-061).
- Reviewer sign-off or "pending" recorded (R-041).
